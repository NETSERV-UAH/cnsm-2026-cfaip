# Rights, licenses and public data

## Existing repository license

The existing GNU General Public License version 3 in [LICENSE](LICENSE) is unchanged. New maintenance scripts and documentation follow that repository license.

Submitted PDFs, videos, original model outputs and imported experiment materials have separate provenance. Their inclusion does **not** assign them a new GPL or Creative Commons license. Existing notices remain in place; where no reuse license is stated, this archive does not invent one.

## Submission agreements and publication

The four form declarations cover AI-generated content without manual text editing, a disclosure, experimental website hosting for accepted papers outside the main proceedings and IEEE Xplore, and presentations for accepted papers. They do not specify a general CC BY grant or explicit public redistribution terms for every non-selected paper, review and video.

Before making the archive public, the organisers should confirm that publication of **all 21 PDFs, all reviews and all 11 videos**, including non-selected work, is covered by permissions they hold. The supplied records alone do not establish those broader permissions. Archive preparation did not obtain new permissions or contact participants.

Declare the actual applicable licenses and their scope in Zenodo. Do not accept a blanket default license unless it matches those permissions. Zenodo supports custom and mixed-license descriptions; see the [publication guide](docs/publication-guide.es.md). The draft metadata leaves the archive-level license unset.

## Personal information

Public metadata excludes contact-email fields, private delivery links, administrative timestamps and form-account identifiers. Private notifications, recipient indexes and temporary exports are omitted.

Names and affiliations are included for participation credit. **Original PDFs may contain author contact details and bylines.** They are preserved as submitted, not described as anonymised. Video content has not been comprehensively screened for embedded personal information. Automated checks do not certify media or every source artifact as free of personal data.

If a derivative must remove information, retain a private original, label the derivative, document the transformation and update its hash. Do not silently change a submission while calling it an original.

## Attribution

The [participant list](PARTICIPANTS.md) identifies recorded submitters, not human authorship of every technical sentence. Credit the artifact's own declared contributors as well as the archive. An archive DOI is a persistent reference, not validation of scientific claims, AI-generated citations or a separate DOI for each paper.
