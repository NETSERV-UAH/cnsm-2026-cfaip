## Names recorded within the papers

The original PDFs retain their own bylines and acknowledgements. These differ from the form’s submitter field. For example, Alberto Leon-Garcia appears alongside David Luo in papers 20 and 21; Benjamin Grando Moreira is named as academic advisor in paper 12. The paper 17 byline also contains “Vilena Vasquez” and “Juno Patinhas”, whose roles are not resolved by the submission form. They are preserved in the PDF and not silently classified as human submitters.

The participant table is a complete index of the supplied submitter field, not a replacement for PDF bylines or a verified list of every human contributor.
